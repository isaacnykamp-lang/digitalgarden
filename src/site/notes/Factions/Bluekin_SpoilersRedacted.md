*"Once in ages forgotten, the apostles of the ███ sought to use their mastery of magics to eliminate their creation- only to find the Children of the Moon had grown so wise that their guile had outstripped their progenitors. While the young of the generation forget so quickly, the Bluekin remember. The children of the Moon will not bear the scars of our people.*"

A race of synthetic nomads grouped together not by species, but the tone of their furs and the protruding singular horns on their heads. They contend this is the source of their affinity towards magics. Practicers of divination, in tune with the dim stars, and keepers of memories long passed. 
Their contention with being perceived is a hallmark of their kind. Older Bluekin insist on meeting strangers in a mask and often repeat mantras about the power that sight holds- for the same reason, they do not give the un-assimilated their real names. 
Masks for wearing have fallen out of fashion with the youth, becoming more of a pastime of antiquity, as well as names. The elders urge their young to shed off these mantras- the Keepers will remember so you don't have to.
All the reasons for their peculiarities can be found with their cryptic [[Memory Keepers\|Memory Keepers]], elders tasked with a codex of information that is only divulged in parable to their fellows. Their knowledge, along with the oracles of the [[Moonpath\|Moonpath]] ensure that true Seekers are rarely necessity in the old tribes. 

The Bluekin have an excellent sense of direction, and frequently travel by night- refusing to do so in complete darkness of the new moon. Once again attributed to their horn by most. Doubtless an effect of their astral gift is their magic sense, a sort of synesthesia that can manifest in the ability to smell, hear and touch magic. The young and gifted would attune this focus with their Memory Keepers. 

Among many eccentricities is their proximity to stones- old gems of their own making, [[Memory Stones\|Memory Stones]] can store information and matter in a state of perpetual stasis- preserving artifacts far beyond their natural lifetimes. The Bluekin are handy with Sunstone, using it and a cadre of fungus to commune with the future and past in divination rituals. Of all races in Arta, the elders of the Bluekin may have the most concrete answers for the Echo phenomenons.

A raucous and passionate group by nature, the young tend to strike off on their own with small bands looking to make their way in the world.

Exiles who have abandoned the Moonpath in search of greater power, however, may be stripped of their horn, and without it their fur becomes blemished and dark. The avaricious of this sect often remove their own eyes- robbed of their attunement, they search for power by way of the serpent. Sight is love, but the unseen is power.


*The Bluekin were early synthetics designed by the ████, and subject to experimentation in █████. The knowledge of a face and a name, or their entire lineage could be manipulated into never having existed by the machinations of the early ████, an ethnocide of forgetfulness that can still be found in the mechanisms of Memory Stones, and the scars of their traditions. The early Bluekin learned to walk by night, hide their faces and tell no names- and in dire need, place themselves out of █████- after all, you can't erase what doesn't exist. In their early distress, they learned to see beyond the present- underneath the soft love of the Moon, the children dreamed of a world where memory was a privilege long forgotten.*
